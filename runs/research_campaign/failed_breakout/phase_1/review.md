## Main findings

The original moving-midpoint exit was the dominant weakness. With one-bar confirmation and efficiency<0.4, removing that exit at a24bar fixed horizon increased5passes to9; shortening the fixed horizon to12bars reduced that to3. A48bar horizon retained9passes but changed the passing asset set materially. Five instruments pass under both24and48bar symmetric variants: AUD, SPX500, UK100, US30 and WTICO. This asset-set sensitivity argues for horizon-neighbor checks in stage2.

Entry selectivity often improved raw positivity but failed the actual opportunity requirement. Strict EMA60 alignment gave10positive instruments and no passes;25%range penetration gave11positive instruments and no passes. Even10%penetration left every positive instrument with at least one missing complete month. Do not count those as successful monthly strategies.

Directional attribution is material. On the48bar branch, long-only round19 has15positive instruments and a+2.96%/year median across all21, but six positive instruments miss complete months: AUD_USD, JP225_USD, NAS100_USD, SPX500_USD, UK100_GBP, US30_USD. It therefore ties at9passes with a lower median passing score than the symmetric winner. Short-only round20 has only1pass, USD_CAD, and a negative median-all score. The symmetric winner and long-only alternative pass different instrument sets; combining entry sides changes which trades are admitted because one trade per instrument remains in force.

## Time concentration and limitations

5/9 winner passes become nonpositive after excluding their best reported calendar-year bucket: CN50_USD, GBP_USD, NZD_USD, USD_CAD, WTICO_USD. This is a descriptive fragility check rather than an extra acceptance gate. Calendar-year buckets include partial endpoints such as2026.

9/9 passes have at least one losing reported year. Detailed yearly values and every zero-entry month remain in each round record.

The boundaries roll each bar. Confirmation checks do not freeze the original swept level, and midpoint exits themselves move as old bars leave the24bar window. No future-confirmed pivots or inferred intrabar ordering are used. Quote activity was tested as a provenance-preserving proxy, not as full-market traded volume.

## Guidance for stage 2

1. Keep the no-midpoint-exit branch as the primary exit hypothesis. Test nearby36/42/54/60bar fixed horizons with explicit per-instrument stability review, rather than selecting by the single highest asset score.
2. Explore long-only frequency recovery around round19 using modest efficiency relaxation or a recent-event window with confirmation. Preserve the sweep/rejection mechanism and keep all21instruments; do not solve monthly gaps by removing difficult assets.
3. Retain a symmetric comparison because the mandated monthly criterion favors it on some assets. A long-only winner must disclose its six positive-but-inactive instruments until their gaps are genuinely repaired.
4. Test whether efficiency and confirmation are necessary through targeted ablations; do not restore restrictive range penetration or EMA-side gates without evidence that monthly activity survives.
5. Report both the declared ranking winner and the stronger median-all alternative. Later cost/horizon diagnostics should remain descriptive and must not silently change acceptance rules.
